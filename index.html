A few good questions.

Do I have to pay a 6% real estate commission?

No fixed commission rate is required by law. Real estate compensation is negotiable. Selling directly can reduce commission costs, but any existing brokerage agreements, agreed buyer-agent compensation, and professional or closing fees still matter. OpenHome does not guarantee a particular saving. Learn about negotiable compensation ↗

Do I have to buy an upgrade to list my home?

No. The planned basic listing is free. Featured placement and marketing materials are optional extras.

Can I pay or subscribe today?

Not yet. These are proposed launch plans, and checkout is not connected. The demo listing form saves only in your browser; no payment or subscription is created.

Does a plan include a broker, an MLS listing, or closing services?

No. These plans cover OpenHome Market listing and advertising services only. Any broker, attorney, title company, appraiser, or inspector would be hired separately under their own agreement and fees.

Will a paid business profile guarantee customers?

No. Businesses pay for advertising visibility. Customers decide who to contact, and no leads, jobs, or closings are guaranteed. Final advertising rates and terms will be confirmed before launch.

What other costs should I expect?

Optional professional services, inspection, appraisal, title, closing, financing, photography, and advertising costs may still apply. The full price and any applicable taxes will be shown before any future purchase.
